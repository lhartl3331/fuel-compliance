Fuel Compliance

Keeps a fuel station’s compliance paperwork in one place on the store PC. Works on Windows 10 and 11.

Download Fuel Compliance Setup
  1. Click the button above.
  2. When the download finishes, click Open file.
  3. Paste the license key from your welcome email when Fuel Compliance asks.

The installer is not code-signed yet. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway. In the browser downloads list, choose Keep if it asks.

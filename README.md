# Fuel Compliance

Fuel Compliance keeps a fuel station's compliance paperwork in one place on the store PC: permits, insurance, tank and line tests, monthly checklists and training records. It scans and files documents, shows at a glance what is current and what is expired or missing, and emails reminders before things come due.

Runs on Windows 10 and 11. Installs just for you, with no administrator needed. Your documents stay on your PC.

## Download

**[⬇ Download Fuel Compliance (Windows)](https://github.com/lhartl3331/fuel-compliance/releases/download/v1.0.0/FuelCompliance-TestKit.zip)**

Unzip it, double-click `FuelComplianceSetup.exe`, and follow the setup wizard. The README inside the zip walks you through each step. You need the license key from your welcome email.

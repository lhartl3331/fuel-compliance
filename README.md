# Fuel Compliance

Fuel Compliance keeps a fuel station's compliance paperwork in one place on the store PC: permits, insurance, tank and line tests, monthly checklists and training records. It scans and files documents, shows at a glance what is current and what is expired or missing, and emails reminders before things come due.

Runs on Windows 10 and 11. Installs just for you, with no administrator needed. Your documents stay on your PC.

## Download

**[⬇ Download Fuel Compliance for Windows](https://github.com/lhartl3331/fuel-compliance/releases/download/v1.0.0/FuelComplianceSetup-1.0.0.exe)**

When the download finishes, click **Open file**. Fuel Compliance installs and opens the setup wizard. Paste the license key from your welcome email when it asks.

The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn you first. If you see "Windows protected your PC", click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
